Problem Statement & Background

In large institutional campuses—such as engineering colleges, university hostels, medical centers, corporate IT parks, and multi-tenant complexes—facility maintenance requests (electrical outages, HVAC compressor failures, plumbing leakages, and broken equipment) are frequently handled through manual paper logs, informal phone calls, or unmonitored WhatsApp groups.

Limitations of Existing Traditional Systems:

  • No Real-time Visibility: Requesters cannot check who is handling their complaint or estimated completion time.
  • Technician Workload Imbalance: Admins lack visibility into active task distribution across trades.
  • Absence of Accountability: No digital timestamped audit trail of when tasks were assigned, started, or resolved.
  • No Post-Resolution Feedback: Facility management cannot gauge user satisfaction or repeat failure rates.
Proposed Solution & Objectives

FixMate provides a unified, responsive enterprise web application designed to streamline facility maintenance end-to-end through a structured 5-stage lifecycle:

1
SUBMITTED
2
ASSIGNED
3
IN PROGRESS
4
RESOLVED
5
CLOSED

Key Objectives:

  • Digitize ticket intake with automatic unique IDs (FM-2026-XXXX) and priority weighting.
  • Enable administrators to assign specialized technicians with one click based on current workload.
  • Provide technicians with a dedicated mobile-friendly workspace to accept jobs, log parts, and submit resolution summaries.
  • Deliver real-time analytics with Chart.js and client-side CSV spreadsheet exports.
Stage 1: Frontend Engine
  • HTML5: Semantic forms, accessible markup, unique IDs.
  • CSS3: Custom design system, responsive grid/flexbox, dark mode tokens.
  • Vanilla JS: Pure DOM manipulation, live event listeners, client-side filtering.
  • LocalStorage Layer: Modular CRUD interface ready for API swap.
  • Chart.js: Interactive visual dashboards and distribution graphs.
Stage 2: Backend Architecture
  • PHP 8+: RESTful backend endpoints with JSON responses.
  • MySQL Database: Normalized relational schema (fixmate_db).
  • Security Measures: password_hash(), PDO prepared statements, XSS prevention.
  • Session Auth: Server-side role-based access control.
